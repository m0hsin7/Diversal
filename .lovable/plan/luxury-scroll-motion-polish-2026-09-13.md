# Luxury scroll-motion polish

## Direction
- Keep the continuous architectural film and current information architecture.
- Make motion visibly respond to scrolling through refined depth, pacing, and section transitions rather than decorative effects.
- Preserve the cool porcelain, graphite, and restrained teal identity while improving contrast and visual hierarchy.

## Motion treatment
- Add a reusable scroll-linked wrapper that maps each section’s viewport progress to transform and opacity variables.
- Give the hero a slow exit: headline lift, supporting copy separation, scrim shift, and film depth as the next section arrives.
- Add staggered text-line reveals, slight heading parallax, and controlled section fade/scale transitions.
- Make property images move at different speeds within their frames and give each listing a subtle alternating vertical rhythm.
- Strengthen the buying-options transition and pinned process sequence with clearer active-state movement and depth.
- Animate the proof figure from scroll progress, introduce the partner names sequentially, and let the footer rise over the film with a cinematic closing transition.

## Accessibility and performance
- Use transforms and opacity for continuous motion, scheduled through animation frames.
- Disable scroll-linked choreography for mobile and reduced-motion preferences, retaining simple reveals.
- Keep all interactions and existing content unchanged.

## Verification
- Check desktop scrolling across every section, the pinned process, and footer arrival.
- Check mobile and reduced-motion fallbacks, overflow, text contrast, and browser errors.
